# Controlled relationship vocabulary

## Paper genealogy

`EXTENDS`, `GENERALIZES`, `SPECIALIZES`, `USES_RESULT_FROM`,
`REUSES_METHOD_FROM`, `COMPARES_WITH`, `CORRECTS`, `PRECEDES_IN_SERIES`,
`VERSION_OF`

## Concepts and methods

`INTRODUCES`, `DEFINES`, `STUDIES`, `USES`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `ASSUMES`

## Mathematical constructions

`CONSTRUCTS`, `CLASSIFIES`, `CATALOGS`, `ENUMERATES`, `COMPUTES`, `MAPS_TO`,
`DECOMPOSES_INTO`, `PARTITIONS_INTO`, `REPRESENTS`, `ENCODES`, `REALIZES`,
`REDUCES_TO`, `LIFTS_TO`, `EQUIVALENT_TO`, `ISOMORPHIC_TO`, `GENERATED_BY`,
`EQUIVALENCE_CLASS_OF`, `QUOTIENT_OF`

## Claims and evidence

`MAKES_CLAIM`, `REPORTS_RESULT`, `DERIVES`, `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`,
`QUALIFIES`, `REQUIRES_ASSUMPTION`

## Scope and artifacts

`APPLIES_TO`, `USES_GROUP`, `USES_ALGEBRA`, `DESCRIBES_REPRESENTATION`,
`DESCRIBES_MULTIPLET`, `HAS_INPUT`, `HAS_OUTPUT`

## Existing bibliographic relationships

`AUTHORED_BY`, `CITES`, `PART_OF_SERIES`

Dimensions, supercharge counts, color counts and node counts are properties.
They are not graph edges unless later represented by a reviewed scope entity.

## Entity types

`paper`, `concept`, `method`, `invariant`, `algorithm`, `mathematical_object`,
`group`, `algebra`, `representation`, `multiplet`, `atlas`, `claim`, `result`,
`equation`, `scope`, `construction`, `dataset`, `artifact`, `computation`,
`problem`, `theorem`, `operation`, `quantity`, `parameter`, `property`,
`assumption`, `evidence`
