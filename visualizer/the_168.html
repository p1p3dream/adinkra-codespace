      The 168

      You are looking at the S8 permutahedron: all 40,320 permutations of (1..8), each one a point in R^8, every point on the hyperplane sum = 36. The object is genuinely 7 dimensional. The screen shows an exact 3D orthographic slice while it rotates in SO(7).
      1. Drag to steer the 7D rotation. Scroll to zoom.
      2. Click a vertex to read its address. Click a second one to measure the swap distance between them.
      3. Next / Prev walks a nine-beat narrative, from the small S4 solid you can hold, up to the 168 octets this instrument is named after.
